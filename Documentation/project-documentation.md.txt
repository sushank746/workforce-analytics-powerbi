# Workforce Analytics — Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights

## 1. Project Overview

Workforce Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding employee attrition and identifying workforce patterns associated with employee turnover.

The project combines SQL, Python, and Power BI to transform raw employee data into actionable workforce insights.

### Project Workflow

Raw Dataset
→ SQL Analysis
→ Python EDA
→ Power BI Data Modeling
→ Interactive Dashboard
→ Business Insights

---

## 2. Business Problem

Employee attrition can affect workforce stability, productivity, and hiring requirements.

The objective of this project is to answer questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments have higher attrition?
- Which job roles show higher observed turnover?
- Is overtime associated with higher attrition?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- Does business travel show differences in attrition?
- How do tenure and promotion gaps relate to attrition?
- Are there differences in attrition across age, income, and distance-from-home groups?

---

## 7. Key Findings

### Overall Workforce

- Total employees: 1,470
- Employees who left: 237
- Employees retained: 1,233
- Overall attrition rate: 16.12%
- Average age: 36.92 years
- Average monthly income: 6,502.93
- Average years at company: 7.01 years

### Overtime

Employees working overtime show a substantially higher observed attrition rate:

- Overtime: 30.53%
- No overtime: 10.44%

This makes overtime an important workforce indicator to investigate.

### Department

Sales shows the highest department-level observed attrition at approximately 20.6%, followed by HR, while R&D has a lower observed attrition rate.

### Employee Age

Employees who left had a lower average age than employees who stayed:

- Employees who left: approximately 33.6 years
- Employees who stayed: approximately 37.6 years

### Tenure

Attrition differs across employee tenure groups, with early-tenure employees showing notably higher observed attrition.

### Satisfaction

Lower job and workplace satisfaction groups show higher observed attrition rates compared with higher satisfaction groups.

---

## 8. Business Recommendations

### 1. Review Overtime Patterns

Investigate workload distribution, overtime frequency, and team-level workload where elevated attrition is observed.

### 2. Focus on Department-Level Retention

Sales and HR show relatively higher observed attrition. Further investigation into department-specific employee experience and working conditions can help identify retention opportunities.

### 3. Improve Career Progression Visibility

Review promotion timelines and career development opportunities, particularly for employees experiencing longer promotion gaps.

### 4. Monitor Employee Experience

Track job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, work-life balance, and related workforce indicators alongside attrition.

### 5. Focus on Early-Tenure Employees

Develop stronger onboarding, mentoring, and early-career support programs for employees in their initial years at the company.
